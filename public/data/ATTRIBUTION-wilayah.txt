Wilayah administratif Kota Banjarmasin

Source: Cahya DSN, cahyadsn/wilayah_boundaries
Snapshot: 26fce16d15cc1dc53da79a79359373130cae485d
Source repository states that these boundaries follow Kepmendagri No. 300.2.2-2430 Tahun 2025.

Districts: db/kec/wilayah_boundaries_kec_63.sql (filtered to Kota Banjarmasin, code 63.71)
Neighborhoods: db/kel/63/wilayah_boundaries_kel_63.71.sql
Source files updated 2026-07-29 and 2026-08-20, respectively.
Retrieved 2026-10-22. Coordinates converted from [latitude, longitude] to GeoJSON [longitude, latitude].

Map rendering: the five district outlines and outer city outline use the exterior rings of topological unions of the 52 neighborhood polygons, keeping shared borders aligned. The neighborhood layer is filled by district and keeps neighborhood outlines visible.

This data is for map visualization and must not be treated as a certified legal boundary source.
The Bappeda Litbang page supplied with this project lists district and neighborhood websites; it is not the source of these GIS coordinates.
